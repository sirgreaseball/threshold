Works with PDF reports and with tables copied from a portal or a spreadsheet.

Whole numbers, no guessing.

miss = ⌊(100a − P·t) ÷ P⌋

need = ⌈(P·t − 100a) ÷ (100 − P)⌉

a attended, t held, P minimum percent.

Most calculators divide by 0.8 and round. For an 80% minimum that gives the wrong answer in 2,614 of 3,320 cases up to 80 classes. Threshold keeps every step in whole numbers and checks itself against a brute-force count.
